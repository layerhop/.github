# Code of Conduct

LayerHop follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussion on topic. Harassment, personal
attacks, spam and scams (including fake "support" asking for keys or seed phrases) are not
tolerated and lead to removal.

Report conduct issues to **dev@layerhop.com**. Reports are handled confidentially.
